# Cross-Domain Reading List for Trader Performance

**For**: Ygor — discretionary WIN/WDO day trader on B3.
**Compiled**: 2026-06-20.
**Premise**: Trading performance is downstream of physiology, attention, decision quality, nervous-system regulation, identity, and meaning. The 20-book trading-psych canon (`03-trader-psychology/`) **completely missed** these levers. Adjacent disciplines have stronger evidence on what actually moves the needle.
**Filter**: Personal-development is 90% slop. Only books that genuinely change how a trader operates day-to-day made the list.

---

## TL;DR — the 5 to buy first

| #   | Book                                  | Author           | What it actually changes                                                                                                        |
| --- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Why We Sleep**                      | Matthew Walker   | The single highest-EV "mindset" intervention. 30 min of lost sleep amplifies loss aversion 4×. Your canon doesn't mention this. |
| 2   | **Clear Thinking**                    | Shane Parrish    | Reframes the journal around inputs, not outcomes. The decision-system layer Munger taught ad-hoc, codified.                     |
| 3   | **Tiny Habits**                       | BJ Fogg          | Stack rituals on existing cues. Replaces motivation-driven discipline (collapses post-loss) with behavior anchors.              |
| 4   | **How to Think Like a Roman Emperor** | Donald Robertson | The operational Stoic manual. You have Marcus Aurelius + Frankl (meaning); this is the mechanics.                               |
| 5   | **The Comfort Crisis**                | Michael Easter   | Deliberate discomfort recalibrates the stress response. Direct lever on tilt resilience, not abstract EQ.                       |

These 5 are zero-overlap with each other AND with everything in your existing library AND with the trading-book acquisition list (`04-book-acquisition-list.md`).

## By domain

### 1 — Physiology / performance biology

The biggest gap in your canon. None of the 20 trading-psych books address sleep, exercise, glucose, or HRV as direct decision-quality levers.

| Book                   | Author         | Year | Why for a trader                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Why We Sleep** ⭐    | Matthew Walker | 2017 | Sleep loss compromises pattern recognition, amplifies loss aversion, degrades risk calibration. This is the single highest-leverage non-trading book in the entire list. |
| **Outlive**            | Peter Attia    | 2023 | Z2 cardio (>80% of training volume), glucose management, sleep architecture. Apply Ch. 2-5; skip the medical-record-keeping depth. Companion to Walker.                  |
| **The Comfort Crisis** | Michael Easter | 2021 | Deliberate discomfort (cold, fasting, heat, hiking) builds vagal tone and stress-recovery rate. Operational protocols, not philosophy.                                   |
| **The Obesity Code**   | Jason Fung     | 2016 | Insulin/glucose timing explains the "2pm fog" that kills afternoon WIN/WDO sessions. Quick read; testable with CGM.                                                      |

**Skip**: Andy Galpin doesn't have a single canonical book yet — listen to his podcast appearances (Huberman) instead.

### 2 — Attention as a finite resource

You have Goleman's _Foco_. These deepen it.

| Book               | Author      | Year | Why                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ----------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stolen Focus**   | Johann Hari | 2022 | The diagnosis Newport's _Deep Work_ lacks. Quantifies the cost of every tab-switch in decision-quality terms. Structural causes, not willpower failure. |
| **Indistractable** | Nir Eyal    | 2019 | Distinction between external distraction (notifications) and internal distraction (boredom → revenge trade). The internal half is exactly what tilt is. |

### 3 — Habit / identity / behavior change

You don't own _Atomic Habits_. Fix that.

| Book               | Author      | Year | Why                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ----------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Atomic Habits**  | James Clear | 2018 | Identity-based habit design. "I am a trader who journals" > "I will journal." Operationalizes Dweck's growth mindset.                             |
| **Tiny Habits** ⭐ | BJ Fogg     | 2019 | Implementation model: stack new habits on existing anchors (after I open the platform → I write the pre-mortem). Works when motivation collapses. |

Read Fogg first (the _how_), then Clear (the _why_). They're complementary.

### 4 — Stoicism / meaning / drawdown resilience

You own _Meditações_ (Marco Aurélio), _Em Busca de Sentido_ (Frankl), _Em Busca do Tempo Perdido_ — adjacent. These add the **practice manual**.

| Book                                     | Author           | Year | Why                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **How to Think Like a Roman Emperor** ⭐ | Donald Robertson | 2019 | Marcus Aurelius's psychotherapist (literally — Robertson is a CBT therapist). Stoicism rendered as protocols: dichotomy of control, premeditatio malorum, view-from-above. |
| **The Obstacle Is the Way**              | Ryan Holiday     | 2014 | Narrative version. Less protocol, more memorable framing. Use Robertson for mechanics, Holiday for motivation.                                                             |
| **A Guide to the Good Life**             | William Irvine   | 2009 | Modern Stoic practice. Negative visualization specifically applied to financial loss.                                                                                      |

**Skip**: Ryan Holiday's other books (_Ego Is the Enemy_, _Stillness Is the Key_) — diminishing returns.

### 5 — Decision-making under uncertainty

You're about to buy Duke / Klein / Tetlock from the trading-acquisition list. These add quantitative + signal-detection lenses.

| Book                               | Author               | Year | Why                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clear Thinking** ⭐              | Shane Parrish        | 2023 | "Decision system" framework — codifies what Munger taught ad-hoc. Bridges single-decision quality (Duke) to repeated-decision flow (your daily WIN sessions).  |
| **The Signal and the Noise**       | Nate Silver          | 2012 | Bayesian signal detection. Duke teaches reasoning; Silver teaches what's actually signal. Examples in elections and baseball map to chart pattern recognition. |
| **The Almanack of Naval Ravikant** | Eric Jorgenson (ed.) | 2020 | Compound leverage, judgment, skin-in-the-game — Munger for traders in fragment form. Weekly-reread material.                                                   |

**Skip**: _Poor Charlie's Almanack_ (Munger) — dense, expensive, oversized. Read Parrish + Naval instead.

### 6 — Stress / nervous system / emotional regulation

The deepest gap in your canon. Goleman teaches EQ at the cognitive level; this layer is somatic.

| Book                                    | Author              | Year | Why                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Body Keeps the Score**            | Bessel van der Kolk | 2014 | Repeated trading losses dysregulate the nervous system at body-level, not just thought-level. Somatic recovery protocols (breath, movement, rhythm). |
| **The Pocket Guide to the Vagus Nerve** | Stanley Rosenberg   | 2017 | Vagal tone = stress resilience baseline. 30-minute read with field protocols. Pairs with HRV measurement.                                            |
| **Breath**                              | James Nestor        | 2020 | Nasal breathing, CO₂ tolerance, slow respiration as a parasympathetic lever. Practical and fast.                                                     |

### 7 — High-stakes professional discipline

What surgeons, pilots, special forces, elite athletes know.

| Book                                      | Author          | Year | Why                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Peak**                                  | Anders Ericsson | 2016 | The deliberate-practice framework — corrects the "10,000-hour" misreading. Real insight: 10h deliberate > 100h passive. Applies directly to backtest review rituals. |
| **Black Box Thinking**                    | Matthew Syed    | 2015 | Why aviation never repeats fatal mistakes and individuals do. The institutional psychology of post-mortems. Pairs with Gawande's _Checklist Manifesto_.              |
| **An Astronaut's Guide to Life on Earth** | Chris Hadfield  | 2013 | Astronaut training discipline — visualize disaster, train calmness as a skill, control what's controllable. Single-sitting, memorable.                               |

**Skip**: Jocko Willink (_Extreme Ownership_, _Discipline Equals Freedom_) — motivational tone over mechanism; military framing doesn't transfer cleanly to solo trader.

### 8 — Writing / clarity of thought

Your journal quality caps your post-mortem learning rate.

| Book                | Author          | Year | Why                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | --------------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **On Writing Well** | William Zinsser | 2006 | Clarity, brevity, removing clutter. Direct lever on post-trade journal entry quality. Sloppy writing hides unclear thinking, which hides edge leaks. |

**Skip**: _Strunk & White_ — antiquated. _The Sense of Style_ (Pinker) — dense, academic. Zinsser is the one.

### 9 — Solitude / self-reliance

Discretionary trading is structurally solitary. No single great book here.

- **Digital Minimalism** (Newport, 2019) — phone-minimalism protocol. Worth one read.
- _No deeper canonical book exists for solo-operator philosophy that maps to trading._ The closest is Naval's _Almanack_ (already listed in §5).

## Domains where NO good book exists (don't waste search time)

1. **B3-specific physiological performance** — no trader sleep / glucose / HRV studies. Run your own correlation experiment using Axion as the data layer.
2. **Intraday drawdown psychology (peer-reviewed)** — Harris's _Happiness Trap_ covers ACT in general; no trader-specific variant.
3. **Solo-operator philosophy** — Naval's _Almanack_ is closest; no comprehensive book.
4. **Portuguese-language performance physiology / trader psychology** — empty academic set.
5. **Recovery protocols for pattern-recognition fatigue** — _Why We Sleep_ + _Outlive_ + Galpin podcasts are the closest. No unified framework exists.

## Suggested reading order (top 10, full Tier-S sequence)

If you order the top 10 from this file today, read them in this sequence — each lens changes what's salient in the next:

1. **Walker — _Why We Sleep_** (1 week) — install the physiological foundation FIRST. Everything else compounds off this.
2. **Fogg — _Tiny Habits_** (3 days) — build the ritual stack before adding more content.
3. **Parrish — _Clear Thinking_** (5 days) — reframe the journal.
4. **Robertson — _Roman Emperor_** (2 weeks) — Stoic reframe for drawdown.
5. **Easter — _Comfort Crisis_** (1 week) — start cold showers + extended walking.
6. **Clear — _Atomic Habits_** (1 week) — identity-stack on top of Fogg's anchors.
7. **Ericsson — _Peak_** (2 weeks) — restructure backtest reviews as deliberate practice.
8. **Silver — _Signal and the Noise_** (2 weeks) — Bayesian framing for chart reads.
9. **Van der Kolk — _Body Keeps the Score_** (3 weeks) — somatic recovery from losing days.
10. **Syed — _Black Box Thinking_** (1 week) — institutional psychology of post-mortems.

**Then re-read your existing trading-psych canon**. You'll see Mark Douglas through new eyes — Why We Sleep + Tiny Habits + Stoic reframe + somatic recovery transforms how his framework lands.
